# SA-JAS — Demo Video Script (LinkedIn)

**Goal:** Stop the scroll with a real, personal problem (the FNB rejection), show the product actually working, and convey how far the project has come — ending on the mission and a soft CTA.
**Runtime:** ~90 seconds · **Format:** 16:9 screen capture, **burned-in captions** (LinkedIn autoplays muted) · **Tone:** honest, a little defiant, builder-proud.

### Production notes
- **Record edited, not live.** Capture each section separately (OBS / Screen Studio), then cut. A flaky scraper or API hiccup shouldn't tank the take.
- **Scrub before you hit record:** dummy CV + fake name/email, **no real ID number**, a throwaway demo job-board login. **Never show the Settings/keys screen.** **End the apply section at "ready to submit" — do not submit to a real company.**
- **Music:** low, building lo-fi/electronic; lift at the title reveal (Shot 3) and the apply moment (Shot 7).
- **Caption style:** large, high-contrast, bottom third. Keep on-screen text short — it carries the muted view.

## SHOT 7 — Agent 3: the Apply Copilot (the trust moment) (1:02–1:16)

**VISUAL:** A visible browser opens on a **demo** application form. The floating copilot HUD appears. It auto-fills fields, attaches the tailored CV PDF. Cursor moves toward **Submit** — and **stops**. A HUD banner reads "Review and submit — your call." **Freeze the frame before submit.**
**ON-SCREEN TEXT:**
> **3. Apply Copilot** — fills the form, attaches the CV…
> **then stops. It never submits for you. You stay in control.**

**VOICEOVER:** "Agent three rides along in a real browser — fills the form, attaches the right CV — and then it stops. It never submits for me. The final click is always mine. That's the whole point: speed, but a human stays on the loop."

**NOTE:** This is the most important 4 seconds in the video. Hold on the "stops before submit" moment.
